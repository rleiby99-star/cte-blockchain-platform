Canvas Import Instructions

  • Click Export to Canvas CSV above.
  • In Canvas: Gradebook → Import → Upload the CSV file.
  • Each standard/rubric appears as a separate column. Points possible: 100.
  • Letter grades follow Howard County MD scale: A 90–100, B 80–89, C 70–79, D 60–69, F <60.
